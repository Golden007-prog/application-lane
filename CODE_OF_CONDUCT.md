# Code of conduct

Application Lane is used by people looking for work, which is often a stressful time. Treat everyone here (people reporting bugs, contributors, maintainers) the way you would want to be treated while job hunting.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith. Many reports come from people who are not developers.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion on the project.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal details. If a report or comment shows an applicant's résumé, answers, address or contacts, do not quote it; tell a maintainer so it can be removed.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, employment situation or skill level.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information, or pressuring anyone to share their own.
- Using the project's issues, discussions or reports to promote, recruit for or advertise anything.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussion after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

To report a problem in confidence, email the maintainer at nikman70055@gmail.com. Say where it happened (a link is enough) and what happened. Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept private. You will hear back within a week.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks it, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is. This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.
